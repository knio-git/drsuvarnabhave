About the Doctor

Dedicated to your recovery & wellbeing

Dr. Suvarna Bhave is a highly experienced physiotherapist based in Karve Nagar, Pune, with over 15 years of dedicated clinical practice. She holds a Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPTh) and a Master of Physiotherapy (MPTh), and has treated thousands of patients across all age groups.

Her clinic — Dr. Suvarna Bhave Physiotherapy & Rehab — is a trusted destination for comprehensive physiotherapy and rehabilitation services in the Karve Nagar and Tathawade Garden area of Pune.

What We Offer

Comprehensive treatments & therapies

01

Orthopaedic Physiotherapy

Treatment for bone, joint, and muscle conditions including knee pain, back pain, frozen shoulder, and fracture rehabilitation.

02

Neuro Rehabilitation

Specialised rehab for stroke, Bell's palsy, Parkinson's disease, hemiplegia, and other neurological conditions.

03

Sports Physiotherapy

Injury assessment and recovery for ACL tears, tennis elbow, ankle sprains, muscle strains and return-to-sport programmes.

04

Post-Surgical Rehab

Structured recovery programmes after hip/knee replacement, spinal surgery, ACL repair and other orthopaedic procedures.

05

Electrotherapy

IFT, Ultrasound therapy, TENS, SWD and muscle stimulation for pain relief, inflammation reduction and tissue healing.

06

Women's Health & Antenatal

Pre and post-natal physiotherapy, pelvic floor rehabilitation, posture correction and postpartum recovery support.
